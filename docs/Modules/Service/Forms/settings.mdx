# Settings

## Instructions

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1.  In the left sidebar menu, go to ‘Service > Settings’.
2.  On the settings page, adjust the settings as per your requirements and click 'Save'.

| S.No | Setting Name                  | Description |
|------|-------------------------------|-------------|
| 1    | Service Provider Names        | Any school, company, hospital providing service to be entered here |
| 2    | Service Done By Names*        |  |
| 3    | Service Type*                 | What service are you providing? Please enter here. |
| 4    | Service Document Upload Types* | What documents should the customer <br/>or patients bring at the time of service |
| 5    | Service Entry SMS             | The time of entering the service which SMS will be sent, <br/>whether he is concerned or not, it is recorded. |
| 6    | Template ID                   | SMS sent at the time of service entry will be recorded |
| 7    | Template                      | The template for the SMS to be sent at <br/>the time of login to the service will be entered here. |
| 8    | Service Report SMS            | Generate service report after SMS to customer. <br/>Let's go yes/no — this yes/no we can turn on/off |
| 9    | Template ID                   | You need to enter the template ID of the SMS you will send for the service, which is provided with the SMS. |
| 10   | Template                      | At the time of service entry, the client will receive an SMS. <br/>Please enter and proceed. |
