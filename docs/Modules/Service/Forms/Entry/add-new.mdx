# Add Service

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1. On the left sidebar, hover over Service. A submenu will appear. Click on Add New.

  ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/add-service.png)

2. A page will open. Enter the service details and click on Save.

  ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/add-service-2.png)
