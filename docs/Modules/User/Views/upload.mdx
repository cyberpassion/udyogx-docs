# Upload Document

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

### 👤 Navigate to Add User 

1.  You will see the **User icon** on the top-right side.
2.  Click on the **User icon**.
3.  A dropdown menu will appear.
4.  Click on the **User List** option from the dropdown.
5.  In the list, find the student, and click on the filter arrow located next to it.
6.  In the dropdown menu go to 'User > Upload'
7.  On the upload page, choose the document type from the top menu, then drag and drop your file into the designated area and click Upload.
8.  Your file will now be uploaded.
