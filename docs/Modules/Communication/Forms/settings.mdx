# Settings

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1.  In the left sidebar menu, go to ‘Communication > Settings’. 

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Communication-Settings-1.png)

2.  A settings page will appear.
3.  Adjust the settings as per your choice and click on 'Save'.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Communication-Settings-2.png)
