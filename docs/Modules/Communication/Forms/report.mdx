# Coupons Report

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1.On the left sidebar, hover over Subscription. A submenu will appear. Hover over Coupon, then click on Coupons Report.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/coupon-report.png)

2. A page will open. Enter the required filters and click on View Report.
