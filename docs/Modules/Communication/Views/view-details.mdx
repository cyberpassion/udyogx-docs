# How to View Details

You can view details easily using UdyogX.

## Stepwise Guide

1. **Log in** to the software by entering your **username** and **password**.
2. On the left sidebar, hover over Communication. A submenu will appear. Click on View List.
3. A list will be displayed. On the right side of the desired entry, click on the Arrow icon. Then hover over Communication, and from the submenu, click on View Details.
4. A page will open displaying the details.
