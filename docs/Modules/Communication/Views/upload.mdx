# Upload

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1. On the left sidebar, hover over Communication. A submenu will appear. Click on View List.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/upload.png)

2. A list will be displayed. On the desired entry, go to the right side and hover over the Arrow icon. Then hover over Communication and click on Upload.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/upload-2.png)
    
3. A page will open. Upload the required files.
