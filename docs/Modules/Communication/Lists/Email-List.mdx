# Email List

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1.  In the left sidebar menu, go to ‘Communication > View List’.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/email-list.png)

2.  A page will open. At the top, under filters, click on Mode. A dropdown will appear. Select Email.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/email-list-2.png)
    
3.  The email list will be shown.
