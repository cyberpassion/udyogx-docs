# Template List

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1.  In the left sidebar menu, go to ‘Communication > Template > Template List’.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/update-communication.png)
    
2.  Alternatively you can also search Communication in the searchbar at the top.
3.  In the list that appears, find the LIST, and hover on the filter arrow located next to it.
4.  In the template list page, there are Date, Recipient and Mode buttons on the top right side.
5.  You can also quickly view the communication list by selecting your choice from there.
