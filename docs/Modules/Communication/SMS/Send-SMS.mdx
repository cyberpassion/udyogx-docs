# Send SMS

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1.  On the left sidebar, hover over Communication. A submenu will appear. Hover over Send, then from the next submenu, click on Send SMS.
    
    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/send-sms.png)
    
2. A page will open. Enter the required details and click on Send to send the SMS.
