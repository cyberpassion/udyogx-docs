# SMS Templates

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1. In the left sidebar menu, go to ‘Communication > Template > View List’.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/email-list.png)

2. A page will open. At the top, under filters, click on Mode. A dropdown will appear. Select SMS.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/sms-list.png)

3. All the SMS templates will be shown.
