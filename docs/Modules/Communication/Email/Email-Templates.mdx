# Email Templates

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1. On the left sidebar, hover over Communication. A submenu will appear. Hover over Template, then from the next submenu, click on Add New.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/email-templates-1.png)

2. A page will open. Enter the required details and click on Save.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/email-templates-2.png)
