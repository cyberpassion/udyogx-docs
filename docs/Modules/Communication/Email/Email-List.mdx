# Email List

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1.  In the left sidebar menu, go to ‘Communication > EMail List’.
2.  A filter page will open. Select the desired filters from the available options and click 'View Report'.
3.  The report will be displayed on the page.
4.  To print the report, click the 'Print' button or press CTRL+P.
