# Send Email

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1. Log in to the software entering your username and password.
2. On the left sidebar, hover over Communication. A submenu will appear. Hover over Send, then from the next submenu, click on Send Email.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/send-email.png)
    
3. A page will open. Enter the required details and click on Send to send the email.
