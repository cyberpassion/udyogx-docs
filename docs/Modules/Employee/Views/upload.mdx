# Upload Document

Follow these steps to Upload Employee Documents in UdyogX.

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1.  In the left sidebar menu, go to 'Employee > Employee List'.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Employee-List.png)

2.  In the list, find the Employee's name, and click on the filter arrow located next to it.
3.  In the dropdown menu click on 'Employee > Upload'.
    
    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Upload-Employee.png)
    
4.  The Upload page will open. From the top menu select the document you wish to upload.
5.  Click on the cloud button and then select the document you wish to upload.
6.  The document will be uploaded successfully.
