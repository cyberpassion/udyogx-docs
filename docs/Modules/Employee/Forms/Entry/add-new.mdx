# Add Employee

## Stepwise Guide

Follow these steps to Add New Employee in UdyogX.

## Video Guide

## Stepwise Instructions

Log in to the software entering your username and password

1.  In the left sidebar menu, go to ‘Employee> New Employee’.![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/2024-09-14_15-54-05-300x150.png)
2.  A form will open where you can fill the employee details.
3.  Fill out the basic details such as 'Employee Name',  'Employee Number', 'DOB', 'Employee Email' etc. Fields marked \* are mandatory to fill.
4.  After filling the details click on 'Save' button at the bottom.![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/New-Employee-Entry-Save-300x200.png)
5.  You can see the new employee would show in the employee List.
