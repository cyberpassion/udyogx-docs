# Update Survey

**Follow these Steps to Update a Survey**

1.  Log into the Software
2.  Hover on "Survey" module visible on the left sidebar menu.
3.  Click on “View List” submenu that appears.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Survery-View-List-300x200.png)

4.  In the Survey Entry Table, find the Survey name, and hover on the arrow located at right side of the Survey.
5.  Hover on submenu “Survey” then submenu "Edit" that appears.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Survery-ViewList-Edit--300x200.png)

6.  In the form that appears, update the necessary details like “Category”, “Date”, “Question”, “Options”, “Why Survey”.
7.  Click on ‘Save’ button at the bottom.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Survery-Entry-300x200.png)
    
8.  A confirmation box will appear when the details are saved successfully.

By following these steps, you will successfully Update a Survey in the Survey module.
