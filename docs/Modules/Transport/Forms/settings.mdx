# Settings

## Stepwise Guide

1. **Log in** to the software by entering your **username** and **password**.
2.  In the left sidebar menu, go to ‘Transport > Settings’.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/transport-settings.png)

3.  Adjust the settings as per your choice and click on 'Save'

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/transport-settings-2.png)
