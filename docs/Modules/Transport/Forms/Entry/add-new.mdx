# Add Transport

You can add new transport easily using UdyogX.

## Stepwise Guide

1.  **Log in** to the software by entering your **username** and **password**.
2.  In the left sidebar menu,, hover on Transport, then click on New Vehicle Entry.

  ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/add-transport.png)

3.  You will see the Vehicle Entry Form.
4.  You will enter vehicle details in this form like Route Name and Vehicle Detail, Driver Details and other details. Star (\*) marked entries are mandatory.
5.  After making the entries, you click on the 'Save' button given at the bottom. Doing so will save your entries and a confirmation block will appear.

  ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/add-transport-2.png)
  
6.  On successful entry, a confirmation SMS will be sent to the driver.

Note:

1.  If some entries are not available with you, then you can update after saving.
2.  To send SMS it is necessary that credits are available in your account.
