# Report

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1.  In the left sidebar menu, go to ‘Student > Student List Report’.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Student-Report-1.png)

2.  Select the session and type of report you want. You can also choose specific filters as per your requirement
3.  For more filters you can click on 'Show More Filters' at the bottom
4.  From the list on the right, please choose the details you wish to include in your report.
5.  Click on 'View Report' after selecting the filters

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Student-Report-2.png)
    
6.  The student report will appear on the page. You can print the report by clicking on Print button or CTRL+P
