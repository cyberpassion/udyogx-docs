# Update Examresult

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1.  In the left sidebar menu, go to 'Exam > View Result List'.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Examresult-List.png)

2.  In the list, find the Exam , and hover on the filter arrow located next to it.
3.  A dropdown menu would appear. Select 'Examresult > Add/Update'.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Update-Examresult.png)
    
4.  The entry page will open where you can make changes.
5.  Upload Excel Sheet with Marks of Students and then click on the student whose marks need to be updated.
6.  You can also update student's marks by searching his/her name on the login page.
5.  After making the changes click on 'Save' button at the bottom.
6.  A confirmation modal would appear on Result Updated.
7.  Once you click on Save button, you can view Examresult > Report-Card by clicking on View button below the updated result.
