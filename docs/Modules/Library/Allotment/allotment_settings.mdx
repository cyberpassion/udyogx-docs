# Allotment Settings

You can update allotment settings easily using UdyogX.

## Stepwise Guide

1. **Log in** to the software by entering your **username** and **password**.
2. On the left sidebar, hover over Library. A submenu will appear.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/allotment-settings.png)

3. Hover over Settings, then click on Allotment Settings.
