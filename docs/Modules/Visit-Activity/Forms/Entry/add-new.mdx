# Add Visit Activity

Follow these steps to Add New Visit Activity in UdyogX.

## Stepwise Guide

1.  **Login to Account:** Access your account by entering your credentials on the login page.
2.  **Navigate to Module:** Hover over the relevant module, in this case, "Visit Activity", and click on _"Add New"_.
3.  **Upload Excel Option:** On the top-right corner of the screen, click on _"Upload Excel"_.
4.  **Download Sample Sheet:** A popup window will appear. Click on the _"Download"_ button to obtain the sample Excel template.
5.  **Prepare Excel File:** Open the downloaded Excel sheet. Remove any existing sample data, but retain the column headers.
6.  **Enter Visit Data:** Fill in the required fields with the appropriate visit activity data.
7.  **Save the File:** Once all data is entered, save the Excel file on your system.
8.  **Upload Excel File:** Repeat Steps 2 and 3. In the popup window, click _"Choose Files"_, select your updated Excel file, and then click _"Upload"_.
9.  **Upload Complete:** Your visit activity data will be successfully uploaded.
