# Add Leave Application

## Stepwise Guide

You can apply for leaves by following these steps:

1.  Log in to the software using your username and password.
2.  In the left sidebar menu, go to ‘Leave Application > Add New’.![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Apply-for-Leave-Application-Step-2.png)
3.  Select the date you want to take a leave:
    *   For a full-day leave, click in the middle of the date.
    *   For a second-half leave, click the right side of the date until it turns red.
    *   For a first-half leave, click the left side of the date until it turns red.
4.  Enter the reason for your leave in the text area at the bottom.
5.  After writing the reason, click 'Apply'.
6.  A confirmation modal will appear upon successful submission.
