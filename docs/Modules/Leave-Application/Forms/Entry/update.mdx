# Update Leave Application

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1.  In the left sidebar menu, go to ‘Leaves > View List’.   
2.  In the list, find the the leave, and hover on the filter arrow located next to it.
3.  A dropdown menu would appear. Select 'Leaveapplication > Edit'
4.  The list of all students/employees will appear along with options to mark their attendance.
5.  Select the date for the absence:
    *   Click in the middle of the date for a full-day leave.
    *   Click the right side of the date until it turns red for a second-half leave.
    *   Click the left side of the date until it turns red for a first-half leave.
6.  Choose the type of leave and specify whether it was paid or unpaid from the options available at the bottom.
7.  After entering the details, click the 'Apply' button at the bottom.
