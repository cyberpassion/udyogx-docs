# Update Meeting Manager

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1.  In the left sidebar menu, go to 'Meetingmanager > View List'.
    
    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Meetings-List.png)

2.  In the list, find the Meeting, and click on the filter arrow located next to it.
3.  In the dropdown menu, click on 'Meetingmanager > Edit'
    
    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Update-Meetings.png)
    
4.  The entry page will open, where you can make the required changes.
    
5.  After making the changes click on 'Save'

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Meeting-Entry-2.png)

6.  A confirmation modal would appear on successful submission.
