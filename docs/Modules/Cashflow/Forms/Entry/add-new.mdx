# Add Cashflow

Follow these steps to Add New Cashflow entry in UdyogX.

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1.  In the left sidebar menu, go to ‘Cashflow > Expense/Income > Add New Expense/Add New Income’. 

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Add-Expense.png)

2.  A form will open where you have to enter cash details such as ‘Head’, ‘Date’, ‘Amount’, ‘Mode’, ‘Name’, ‘Remark’.
3.  Fill the details and click on ‘Save’.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Add-Expense-2.png)

4.  Now your cash transaction will be recorded.
