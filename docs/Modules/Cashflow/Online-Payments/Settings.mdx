# Settings

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1.  In the left sidebar menu, go to ‘Cashflow > Online-Payment-Settings’.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Online-Payments-Settings.png)
    
2.  Select the settings as per your needs and click 'Save' button.
