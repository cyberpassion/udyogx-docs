# View Income

With udyogX you can easily view list an income. To do this first log in to the software entering your username and password.

## Stepwise Guide

1.  In the left sidebar menu, go to 'Income > View Income List'.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Income-List.png)
    
2.  Alternatively you can also search income in the searchbar at the top.
3.  In the list that appears, find the Income, and hover on the filter arrow located next to it.
