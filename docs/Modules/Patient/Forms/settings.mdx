# Settings

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1.  In the left sidebar menu, go to ‘Patient > Settings’.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Patient-Settings-1.png)

2.  Adjust the settings as per your choice and click on 'Save'

    | S.No | Setting Name                         | Description |
    |------|--------------------------------------|-------------|
    | 1    | Patient Types\*                      | What is the type of patient and his/her entry in staff like management, account staff, doctor, nurse etc. |
    | 2    | Patient Health Card Types            | List of poor health card names that are valid or given by the government, comma included. |
    | 3    | Document Uploads\*                   | What documents are required at the time of patient's entry. |
    | 4    | Columns in Patient Report\*          | Which columns are needed in the patient's report so that their report comes properly? |
    | 5    | Treatment Observation Levels\*       | At the stage of improvement in treatment, when a doctor comes for treatment and examines the patient, it tells what is the progress of the patient. In this we will put in a big way. |
    | 6    | Send Welcome SMS to Patient Automatically | On the entry of any new patient, he will automatically be sent welcome messages from the side of the hospital, his entry will be done. |
    | 7    | SMS Template ID                      | Enter the ID of the person who will send the SMS template. |
    | 8    | Patient Welcome SMS Template\*       | On the entry of any new patient, he has to enter the welcome SMS information from the hospital, which we have to send. |
