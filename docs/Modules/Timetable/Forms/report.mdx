# Report

You can easily export timetable report using UdygoX. Below are the steps,

1.  Firstly follow [the link](https://learn.udyogx.in/docs/view-announcement-report/) to view timetable report.
2.  **Export Timetable Report to PDF:** Click on the 'PDF' export button at the top to export report in PDF. You may also view the pdf clicking view pdf button besides export button.![export-report-to-pdf-udyogx](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/export-report-to-pdf-udyogx.png)
3.  **Export Timetable Report to Excel/CSV:** Click on the 'Excel' export button at the top to export report in Excel.![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/export-report-to-excel.png)
