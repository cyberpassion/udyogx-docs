# Report

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1.  In the left sidebar menu, go to ‘Registration > View Report’.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Registration-Report-1.png)
    
2.  A filter page will appear. Select the filters according to your needs and click on 'View Report'

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Registration-Report-2.png)

3.  A report page will open with all the details.
4.  Click the 'Print' button or press CTRL+P to print the report.
5.  If you wish to add or remove any columns in the report, you can find the 'Add/Remove Columns' option at the top of the screen.
    
    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Registration-Report-3-1.png)

6.  Click on it, and a popup will appear. Tick the columns you wish to include in the report and click 'Save and Proceed'.
