# Add Registration

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1.  In the left sidebar menu, go to ‘Registration > Add New’.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Registration-Entry-1.png)

2.  A form will open where you have to enter Registration's details.
3.  Fill the necessary details such as ‘Type’, ‘Date’, ‘Name’, ‘Phone Number’, ‘Email’,  ‘Address’, ‘Remark (if any)’ and others. Fields marked \* are compulsory.
4.  After entering the details click on ‘Save’ at the bottom.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Registration-Entry-2.png)
    
5.  A confirmation modal would appear on successful submission.
