# Settings

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1.  In the left sidebar menu, go to ‘Customer > Settings’.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Customer-Settings-1.png)

2.  Adjust the settings as per your needs and click on 'Save'
    
    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Customer-Settings-2-1-1.png)
