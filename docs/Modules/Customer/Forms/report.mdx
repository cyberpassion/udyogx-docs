# Report

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1.  In the left sidebar menu, go to ‘Customer > View Report’.![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/How-To-View-Customer-Report-Step-1.png)
2.  Choose the filters as per your need and click on 'View Report'![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/How-To-View-Customer-Report-Step-2.png)
3.  The report will display on the screen. Click on 'Print' button or press CTRL+P
