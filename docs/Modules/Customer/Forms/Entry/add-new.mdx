# Add Customer

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1.  In the left sidebar menu, go to ‘Customer > Add New’.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/How-To-Add-New-Customer-Step-1.png)
    
2.  A form will open where you have to enter student details.
3.  Fill the necessary details such as ‘Customer Name’, ‘Customer Type’, ‘Phone Number’, ‘Email’, ‘Address’ and others. Fields marked \* are compulsory.
4.  After entering the details click on ‘Save’ at the bottom.![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/How-To-Add-New-Customer-Step-4.png)
5.  A confirmation modal would appear on successful submission.
