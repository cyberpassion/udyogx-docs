# View Details

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1. On the left sidebar, hover over Subscription. A submenu will appear. Hover over Coupon, then click on Coupons List.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/upload-docs.png)

2. A list will appear. For the desired coupon, go to the right side and hover over the Arrow icon. Then hover over Coupon and click on View Details.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/view-details-2-1.png)
    
3. A page will open displaying the coupon details.
