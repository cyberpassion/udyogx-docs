# Report

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1.  In the left sidebar menu, go to ‘Booking > View Report’.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Booking-Report.png)

2.  Select the filters as per your needs and click on 'View Report'

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Booking-Report-1.png)

3.  The report will show on the page. Click on 'Print' button or press CTRL+P to print the report
