# Update Visit Planner

**Follow these Steps to Create New Visit with Excel/CSV in UdyogX**

## Stepwise Guide

1. Log into the software.
2. On the left sidebar, hover over Visit Planner. A submenu will appear. Click on View List.

  ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/update-visit-planner.png)

3. A page with all the visit planners will be shown. On the desired entry, hover over the arrow on the right, then hover on Visit Planner, and click on Edit.

  ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/update-visit-planner-2.png)

4. A page will open. Make the required updates and hit Save.

  ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/update-visit-planner-3.png)
