# Export Report Data

## Stepwise Guide

1.  Log in to the portal using your credentials.
2.  After logging in, the **Dashboard** page will appear.
3.  To view the list of any module, click on the respective module from the dashboard menu.
4.  In the left sidebar menu, go to 'Student > Student List Report' page.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Screenshot-07-27-2024-13.33.03-300x150.png)

5.  Select the session and type of report you want. You can also choose specific filters as per your requirement
6.  For more filters you can click on 'Show More Filters' at the bottom
7.  From the list on the right, please choose the details you wish to include in your report.![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Screenshot-07-27-2024-13.36.02-300x150.png)
8.  Click on 'View Report' after selecting the filters
9.  The student report will appear on the page.
10. **Export Student Report to PDF:** Click on the 'PDF' export button at the top to export report in PDF. You may also view the pdf clicking view pdf button besides export button.
11. **Export Student Report to Excel/CSV:** Click on the 'Excel' export button at the top to export report in Excel.
12.  The same process can be followed in any module to export report data through the **Student List Report** section.
