# Add Product

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1.  In the left sidebar menu, go to ‘Product > Add New’.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Product-Entry-1.png)

2.  A form will open where you have to enter Product's details.
3.  Fill the necessary details such as ‘Category’, ‘Brand’, ‘Product Name’, ‘Unit Price’, ‘Retail Price’ and others. Fields marked \* are compulsory.
4.  After entering the details click on ‘Save’ at the bottom.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Product-Entry-2.png)

5.  A confirmation modal would appear on successful submission.
