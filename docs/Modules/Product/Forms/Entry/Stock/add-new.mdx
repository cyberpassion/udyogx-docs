## Stepwise Guide

Let's say you have created a product. To keep track of the stock for this product, you can make an entry by following these steps:

1.  Log in to the software entering your username and password.
2.  In the left sidebar menu, go to ‘Product > Stock > Add New’.

   ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/product-stock-add-new-300x200.png)

3.  A form will open where you have to enter details of the product.
4.  Fill the necessary details such as ‘Date’, ‘Product’, ‘Quantity’, ‘Supplier Price’, ‘Supplier’ and others. Fields marked \* are compulsory.
5.  Once all the details are filled in, click the 'Save' button at the bottom.

   ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/stock-entry-page-300x200.png)
   
6.  A confirmation modal would appear on successful submission.
