# Stock Report

## Stepwise Guide

You can check the product report by following these steps:

1.  Log in to the software entering your username and password.
2.  In the left sidebar menu, go to ‘Product > Stock > View Report’.

  ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/stock-report.png)

3.  A page will open where you can select from various filters. Choose the filters that suit your needs and click on 'View Report'.

  ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/stock-report-2.png)
  
4.  A report page will open with all the stock details. Click the 'Print' button or press CTRL+P to print the report.
