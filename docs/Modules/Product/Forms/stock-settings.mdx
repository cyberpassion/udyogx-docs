# Stock Settings

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1.  In the left sidebar menu, go to ‘Product >Stock > Settings’.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/stock-settings.png)

2.  Choose the settings as per your needs and click on 'Save'

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/stock-settings-2.png)
