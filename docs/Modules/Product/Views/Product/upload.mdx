# Upload Document

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1.  In the left sidebar menu, go to ‘Product > View List’.
    
    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Product-List.png)
    
2.  In the list, find the product, and click on the filter arrow located next to it.
3.  In the dropdown menu go to 'Product > Upload'

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Upload-Product.png)

4.  On the upload page, choose the document type from the top menu, then drag and drop your file into the designated area and click Upload.
5.  Your file will now be uploaded.
