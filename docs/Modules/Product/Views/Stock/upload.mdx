# Upload Document

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1.  In the left sidebar menu, go to ‘Product >Stock > View List’.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/stock-list.png)

2.  In the list, find the student, and click on the filter arrow located next to it.
3.  In the dropdown menu go to 'Stock > Upload'

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/upload-document.png)

4.  On the upload page, choose the document type from the top menu, then drag and drop your file into the designated area and click Upload.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/upload-document-2.png)
    
5.  Your file will now be uploaded.
