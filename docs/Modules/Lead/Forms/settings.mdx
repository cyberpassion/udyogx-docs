# Settings

| S.No | Settings Name                    | Description |
|------|----------------------------------|-------------|
| 1    | Category                         | Lead category means the category in which you are entering a lead, such as when you make a direct call to a client and categorize it as a direct call. Whenever you make a new lead entry, you will select the appropriate category, such as direct call, and proceed with the entry. Similarly, whenever you enter a new lead, whatever category you select will be referred to as the lead category. <br/><br/>**Enter category names separated by comma** |
| 2    | Default Lead Product             | When a company contacts a new customer, they will enter the information into the lead management system. During this entry process, there is an option to select the product name (the item being sold) from a dropdown menu. The term "Default Lead Product" refers to the situation where a product is pre-selected in the dropdown menu. This pre-selected product name will remain displayed until another product is selected. <br/><br/>**Example:** website, Student Management System, sales management system |
| 3    | Default Lead Contact Mode        | If a company contacts a new customer, we will create an entry to indicate the method of communication (e.g., phone call, email, direct visit, WhatsApp, etc.). <br/><br/>**Example:** Call, Email, Direct Visit, WhatsApp, Other |
| 4    | Progress                         | Indicates the method of communication used with the client. Select from dropdown: <br/><br/>**Example:** Call, Email, Direct Visit, WhatsApp, Other |
| 5    | Standard Reponses                | "Standard Responses" are predefined/template replies used for inquiries or feedback to ensure consistency and save time. <br/><br/>**Example entries:** Client Not Interested, Call Back, Existing Client (comma separated) |
| 6    | Lead Reference                   | Used to mention the person who referred the client. <br/><br/>**Example:** Rajesh referred you to the school principal — enter 'Rajesh'. |
| 7    | Lead Pre Notification Days       | Refers to the number of days in advance a lead should be notified before a scheduled event or activity. Helps boost engagement and planning. |
| 8    | Columns in Lead Report           | Select the column names you want displayed in the lead report. |
| 9    | Lead Thanksgiving SMS            | Select Yes/No to confirm whether a Welcome SMS should be sent after a new lead entry. |
| 10   | Thanksgiving SMS Template ID     | Template ID of the lead thanksgiving SMS to be sent. |
| 11   | Thanksgiving SMS Template        | Template/Content of the lead thanksgiving SMS to be sent. |
| 12   | Lead Followup Reminder SMS       | Select Yes/No if a follow-up reminder SMS should be sent to the person handling the lead. |
| 13   | Lead Followup SMS Template ID    | Template ID of the lead reminder SMS to be sent. |
| 14   | Lead Assignment SMS              | Select Yes/No if an assignment SMS should be sent to the person assigned the lead. |
| 15   | Lead Assignment SMS Template     | Template/Content of the lead assignment SMS to be sent. |
