# Report

Follow these steps to View Lead Report in UdyogX.

## Stepwise Guide

1.  Log into the Software.
2.  Hover on the “Lead” module visible on the left sidebar menu and click on “View Report” submenu that appears.![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/lead-report-option-in-udyogx.png)
3.  Apply applicable filters as needed in form that appear and click on “View” button at the bottom.![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/lead-report-form-udyogx.png)
4.  Report would appear in front of you.![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/lead-report-udyogx.png)
5.  On the top of the Report that appears you can choose to print the report or export to PDF or Excel/CSV. Please follow the [article on exporting lead report](https://learn.udyogx.in/docs/export-lead-report-in-excel-pdf/).
