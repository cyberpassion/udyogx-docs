# How to Jumpclass For Student

You can jumpclass a student easily using UdyogX.

## Stepwise Guide

1. **Log in** to the software by entering your **username** and **password**.
2. On the left sidebar, hover over Student.
3. A dropdown will appear. Click on View List.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/jumpclass-student.png)

4. A list of students will appear. On the right side of a student’s entry, hover over the Arrow icon.
5. A dropdown will appear. Hover over Student.
6. A submenu will appear. Click on Promote.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/jumpclass-student-2.png)

7. Enter the new class details and click on Perform.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/jumpclass-student-3.png)
