# How to Export Backup

You can export backup easily using UdyogX.

## Stepwise Guide

1. **Log in** to the software by entering your **username** and **password**.
2. On the top right corner, hover over the User icon.
3. From the dropdown menu, click on "Generate Backup".

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/export-backup.png)

4. Your backup will be generated.
5. Click on the Download button to download and export.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/download-backup-1.png)
