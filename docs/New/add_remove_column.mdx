# How to Add/Remove Column in Reports

You can jumpclass a student easily using UdyogX.

## Stepwise Guide

1. **Log in** to the software by entering your **username** and **password**.
2. On the left sidebar, hover over the module whose report columns you want to add or remove (for example, Student).
3. A submenu will appear. Click on Report.
4. A page will open. Add the required filters and click on View Report.
5. The report will open. On the top right side, click on Add/Remove Columns.
6. A new page will open. Make the necessary changes and click on Save & Proceed.
