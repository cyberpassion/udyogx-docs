# How to Add Bus Stops

## Stepwise Guide

Log in to the software entering your username and password.

1. On the left sidebar, hover over **Transport** and click on **Stops** from the submenu.  
   ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/how-to-add-stops.png)
2. A new page will open; enter the bus stop details and click **Submit**.
3. The stops will be successfully added to the system.
