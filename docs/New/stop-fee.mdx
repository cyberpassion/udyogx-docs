# Salary Settings

You can update stop fee easily using UdyogX.

## Stepwise Guide

1. **Log in** to the software by entering your **username** and **password**.
2. On the left sidebar, hover over Transport and click on Stops.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/stop-fee.png)

3. A page will open. Add a new stop with its fee or update the fee of an existing stop.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/stop-fee-2.png)
    
4. Click on Save to apply the changes.
