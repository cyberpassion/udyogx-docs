# How to Change Password

You can change password easily using UdyogX.

## Stepwise Guide

1. **Log in** to the software by entering your **username** and **password**.
2. On the top right side, hover over the Profile icon. A dropdown will appear. Click on Profile, then from the submenu, click on Change Password.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Change-Password-1.png)

3. A page will appear. Enter your current password and the new password, then click on Change Password.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Change-Password-2.png)

4. The login page will appear. Enter your new credentials to sign in.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Change-Password-3.png)
