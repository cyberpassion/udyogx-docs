# How to Forgot Password

You can forget password easily using UdyogX.

## Stepwise Guide

1. **Log in** to the software by entering your **username** and **password**.
2. On the login page, click on Forgot Password.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Forgot-Password-1.png)

3. A page will open. Enter your Username/Email and click on Proceed.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Forgot-Password-2.png)

4. You will receive a notification with instructions to update your password.

    ![](https://learn.udyogx.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Forgot-Password-3.png)
